PediaCare-Net v2 — XAI v3.2
=============================

What changed from v3.1
----------------------
1. Uses the model probabilities directly (no double sigmoid).
2. Recovers the actual validation-selected decision threshold with the same
   common.find_threshold() policy used by the evaluation pipeline.
3. A successful counterfactual is now required to:
   - make a clinically reportable input change,
   - remain within cohort p99 plausibility bounds, and
   - CROSS the validation-selected decision threshold.
4. Risk reductions that do not cross the decision boundary are saved only as
   partial-sensitivity diagnostics, not as successful counterfactuals.
5. n_case=0 means ALL validation subjects (one representative window per subject).
6. Heatmaps remain limited to a small representative subset for readability.

Interpretation
--------------
Population IG: all 2,000 sampled validation windows.
Patient-level cases: all validation subjects, one representative window per subject.
Heatmaps: only 6 representative subjects for thesis readability.
Counterfactual success: only cohort-plausible changes that flip the model decision.
Partial sensitivity: meaningful reductions that fail to cross the threshold; these are
NOT counted as successful counterfactuals.

Scientific caveat
-----------------
Counterfactuals are model-based sensitivity analyses. They do not prove that taking
an insulin/carbohydrate action would causally prevent hypoglycaemia, and they are not
treatment recommendations.
