# Research-use disclaimer

PediaCare-Net v2 is research software accompanying an academic thesis. It is
provided for reproducibility, methodological review, and research use.

It is **not a medical device**, has not been validated or approved for clinical
diagnosis or treatment, and must not be used to make patient-care decisions.
Predictions, thresholds, Integrated Gradients attributions, and counterfactual
outputs are model analyses rather than clinical recommendations.

The repository does not distribute pediatric patient records or trained
checkpoints. Access to any external dataset remains subject to the terms,
consent framework, governance, and licence of the dataset provider.
